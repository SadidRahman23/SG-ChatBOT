1. Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide the Service:

Data TypeWhat We CollectWhy
Account InfoEmail address, hashed passwordAuthentication
Chat MessagesMessages sent during your sessionAI responses
Uploaded FilesImages or documents you attachAI analysis
Usage DataRequest timestamps, rate limitingSecurity & abuse prevention

We do not collect your real name, phone number, location, or payment information.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely for:

  • Authenticating your account and keeping it secure
  • Processing your messages and generating AI responses
  • Preventing abuse and enforcing rate limits
  • Improving the reliability of the Service

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your account data (email and hashed password) is stored in a MongoDB database hosted on a secure cloud provider. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — we never store plain-text passwords.

Chat messages are sent to OpenRouter's API for processing but are not permanently stored by SG ChatBOT after your session ends.

🔐

All communication between your browser and our server uses HTTPS encryption. Passwords are bcrypt-hashed with a cost factor of 12.

4. Third-Party Services

SG ChatBOT uses the following third-party services:

  • OpenRouter — routes your messages to AI models. Their privacy policy applies to message processing.
  • MongoDB Atlas — stores account data securely in the cloud.
  • Render — hosts our backend server.
  • Cloudflare Pages — hosts our frontend. Cloudflare may collect anonymous traffic data.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these providers.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

SG ChatBOT uses browser localStorage (not cookies) to store your authentication token and email address locally on your device. This allows you to stay signed in between sessions.

This data never leaves your device unless you are actively using the Service. You can clear it at any time by signing out or clearing your browser data.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data (email and password hash) for as long as your account is active. Chat messages are not permanently stored by our servers after being processed.

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please contact us.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent at any time by stopping use of the Service

To exercise these rights, contact us through the SG ChatBOT application.

8. Children's Privacy

SG ChatBOT is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the SG ChatBOT application or reach out to Mohammed Sadid Rahman directly.
