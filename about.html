✦ About SG ChatBOT

Built for everyone,
powered by AI.

SG ChatBOT is a free, intelligent AI assistant designed to help with anything — from answering questions and writing code to analyzing images and documents.

🤖 What is SG ChatBOT?

SG ChatBOT is a web-based AI assistant powered by advanced large language models via OpenRouter. It is completely free to use and requires no payment or subscription.

Whether you need help with coding, writing, research, math, or just want to have a conversation — SG ChatBOT is here 24/7.

🎯 Our Mission

We believe AI should be accessible to everyone — not just those who can afford expensive subscriptions. SG ChatBOT is and always will be free for everyday use.

Our goal is to provide a fast, reliable, and privacy-conscious AI assistant that genuinely helps people in their daily lives.

⚠️ Limitations

SG ChatBOT is powered by AI and may sometimes produce incorrect, incomplete, or outdated information. Always verify important information from trusted sources.

The AI does not have access to real-time data, cannot browse the internet, and does not remember conversations between sessions.
